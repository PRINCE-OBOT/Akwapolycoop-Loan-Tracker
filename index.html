Membership Requirements

Follow these simple steps to become a member

👤

Staff Verification

You must be a verified staff member of Akwa Ibom State Polytechnic to be eligible for membership in our cooperative society.

📸

Passport Photograph

Submit a recent passport-sized photograph for your membership profile and identification purposes.

✍️

Two Guarantors

Provide two guarantors who are both verified staff members of Akwa Ibom State Polytechnic institution.
